About Me

Who Am I?
I'm a Frontend Developer with 3 years of turning ideas into pixel-perfect, living, breathing interfaces. I don't just write code — I craft experiences that feel as good as they look.
What I Do
My world lives at the intersection of design and logic. I build fast, accessible, and beautifully animated web applications using React and Next.js, give them structure and confidence with TypeScript, and dress them sharp with Tailwind CSS. Every component I write has a purpose. Every pixel has a reason.
How I Think
I believe the best frontend work is invisible — when a user flows through an interface without friction, without confusion, just pure intention meeting great execution, that's when I know the job is done right. I obsess over details others overlook: micro-interactions, loading states, responsive edge cases, and the subtle things that make a user smile without knowing why.
Beyond the Screen
When I'm not deep in a codebase, I'm studying great design, exploring new web technologies, and constantly asking "how can this be better?"   That curiosity is what keeps me growing.
